Black British History Timeline

Black people have been part of British history for nearly 2,000 years. This timeline runs from Roman York to the present day. Every event is linked to the KS3 National Curriculum and to Britain: Migration, empires and the people, c790 to the present day.

How to use it

Filter by key stage or theme, or search for a person or place. Each event has an enquiry question you can use as a starter, a discussion point or an extended-writing prompt.

Words we use

  • Enslaved people, not “slaves”: people come first, not their condition.
  • Enslavers for those who held people in slavery.
  • Uprising or rebellion for organised resistance.

Big themes

Presence, Empire and trade, Resistance, War and service, Migration, Rights and activism, and Culture. They run through every era, which helps with KS4 thematic answers.
